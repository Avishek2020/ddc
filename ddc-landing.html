Why we're built this way

Most projects die in the gap between the design file and the repository. DDC removes the gap. One team scopes it, draws it, writes it, and keeps it running after launch.

Small on purpose

Four to six people on a project. You talk to the people doing the work, not an account layer.

Fixed scope, fixed price

You get a written scope and a number before anyone opens an editor. Change it and we re-quote in writing.

You own everything

Source files, repositories, infrastructure and documentation transfer to you at handover. No lock-in.

How a project runs

Four stages,
in this order

01

Scope

We interview whoever owns the problem, audit what exists, and write the scope. You get a fixed price and a date.

1–2 weeks

02

Design

Direction first, then screens. You see real content in the design, never lorem ipsum, and you review every week.

2–5 weeks

03

Build

The same team writes the code. You get a staging link from week one and watch it fill in, so nothing is a surprise.

4–12 weeks

04

Run

Launch, then 30 days of fixes included. After that you take it in-house or we stay on a retainer. Your call.
